Our Story

Launch 360 was founded by Nicole Nadeau, a 25+ year HR executive who saw firsthand how expensive and complex leadership assessments were. Traditional 360 providers required expensive consultants, mandatory certification, and weeks of setup — putting actionable leadership insights out of reach for most organizations.

Launch 360 was built to change that. Our platform removes every barrier — no certification, no consulting fees, no IT delays. Just simple, powerful 360 feedback that any organization can use.

Meet Nicole Nadeau, Founder

Nicole Nadeau brings over 27 years of executive HR and leadership development experience. She has held senior HR leadership roles at Thomson Financial and other global organizations, where she designed and implemented leadership assessment programs for thousands of leaders.

As an executive coach and leadership consultant, Nicole saw the gap between what organizations needed — simple, accessible 360 feedback — and what legacy providers offered. She founded Launch 360 to democratize leadership insight, making it available to any organization, regardless of budget or internal expertise.

Why We Evolved Launch 360

Transition to a Continuous Feedback SaaS Platform

Launch 360 originally launched as a one-off assessment tool. But we heard the same feedback from customers: "We want to use this more than once every 18 months."

Our enhanced version is our transition to a subscription SaaS platform. Now organizations can run continuous feedback programs — not just annual assessments. With this new offer, you get recurring surveys, aggregate reporting across time, and our exclusive Personal Impact Survey. Same simplicity. More power.

Continuous Growth, Simplified

Subscribing gives you the freedom to run baseline assessments, track progress with quarterly pulse checks, and run targeted developmental surveys without ever calculating individual invoice costs.
