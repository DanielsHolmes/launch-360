A 360-degree feedback survey collects anonymous feedback from multiple perspectives — typically the leader being assessed (self), their manager, peers, and direct reports. This multi-rater approach provides a complete picture of leadership effectiveness, identifying strengths and development areas that might not be visible from a single perspective.

Most Launch 360 surveys include 30-50 questions across 6-8 leadership competencies. However, you can customize the length based on your needs — shorter surveys for pulse checks, longer surveys for comprehensive development programs.

Most raters complete a 360 survey in 10-15 minutes. Our surveys are designed to be comprehensive but not burdensome, with clear questions and intuitive rating scales.
